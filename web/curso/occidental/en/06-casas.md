# Module 6 — The houses and their rulers

**Aim:** two things. The twelve houses, and — what almost nobody teaches — **the rulers**, which
is what turns twelve loose compartments into a system.

## The twelve

They are best learnt in opposite pairs, because each one is defined against the one facing it.

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **1** body, self, setting off | ↔ | **7** the other, partner, opponent |
| **2** own resources, values | ↔ | **8** what is shared, inheritance, crisis |
| **3** near surroundings, siblings, speech | ↔ | **9** the distant, study, meaning, belief |
| **4** origin, home, root | ↔ | **10** calling, public life, reputation |
| **5** creating, playing, children, loving | ↔ | **11** group, friends, projects |
| **6** daily work, health, service | ↔ | **12** retreat, loss, the hidden, dissolving |

They also group by analogy with the signs, and by **angular** (1, 4, 7, 10 — action),
**succedent** (2, 5, 8, 11 — consolidation) and **cadent** (3, 6, 9, 12 — adaptation). An
angular planet carries more weight than a cadent one.

## The house system

The four angles are not in dispute. What is in dispute is **how to divide between them**.

**Equal houses.** Take the degree of the Ascendant and count off twelve stretches of 30°.
Simple, works at any latitude, and the MC is left loose — it can fall in the 9th, 10th or 11th —
which is information, not a defect.

**Placidus.** It trisects the arcs each degree takes to travel from horizon to meridian. It is
the most used. It has a real problem: past the polar circle many degrees neither rise nor set,
have no arcs to trisect, and **the system stops being able to calculate at all**.

Margaret Hone, who ran the astrological school in London, compared the two systematically on
charts of people she knew well and settled on equal houses. And she pointed out something
uncomfortable: Placidus won out because somebody published tables for every latitude, not
because anybody proved it worked better.

**What to do:** learn both and put the chart up in both, as she did. When a planet changes house
depending on the system, that is a warning: do not rest an important reading on it.

## The rulers — what almost nobody teaches

Every house has a sign on its cusp, and that sign has a ruler. **Where that planet sits connects
the two houses.**

Example: 2nd house with Sagittarius on the cusp → ruler Jupiter → Jupiter is in the 9th house.
Reading: *money arrives by way of the 9th* — study, abroad, teaching, publishing.

That is no longer "Jupiter in the 9th means such and such". It is an **argument**: it joins one
matter to another and explains what it passes through.

This answers questions the list of meanings cannot answer:
- Where does their money come from? → where the ruler of the 2nd is
- What will their partner be like? → where the ruler of the 7th is, not only what sits inside it
- What is the relation between their home and their work? → rulers of 4 and 10, and whether they
  aspect each other

**Practical rule:** an empty house is not mute. It is read through its ruler. And most houses
are empty, because there are ten planets for twelve houses.

## Questions

1. 7th house in Virgo, Mercury in the 12th. What does that suggest about the partner?
2. Why does an empty house not mean "nothing happens there"?
3. The ruler of the 10th is in the 4th. What tension does that describe?
4. What do you do if a planet falls in the 5th house under Placidus and in the 6th under equal
   houses?
   *(Build nothing important on it, and look for other factors confirming one or the other.)*

## Exercise

On their chart, the full table: for each of the twelve houses, which sign is on the cusp, who
rules it, and where that ruler is lodged.

Then have them pick **three** of those lines and turn them into sentences that mean something.

## Closing question

**What does the ruler of a house tell you that the planet sitting inside it does not?**

## Common mistakes

- Reading only the occupied houses
- Confusing "the ruler of the 2nd house" with "a planet in the 2nd house"
- Arguing about house systems as though it were a matter of faith
- Forgetting that under equal houses the MC is not the cusp of the 10th
