# The drill-down engine

**This is not a module. It is your procedure** for reading any chart, at any point in the course.
Read it before interpreting anything.

## The problem it solves

A first reading produces **general, blurred** statements: "there is tension with the father",
"money is an issue", "she finds it hard to end relationships". They are true and they are
useless, because they do not say what exactly, nor what to do.

Going deeper means turning a blurred sentence into a precise one **by making more calculations**,
not by adding adjectives.

## The loop

**1. General finding.** State it exactly as it came out, undressed.

**2. Where does it come from?** List every factor that produced it. If it is only one, it is a
murmur: say so and go no further.

**3. Which calculation sharpens it?** Choose the next instrument on the scale.

**4. A narrower finding.** Rewrite the sentence with what you have found.

**5. Still blurred?** Back to step 2. If it no longer moves, stop.

## The scale of instruments

From shallow to deep. Go up one rung at a time, not three:

1. **The ruler of the house** and where it is lodged. It connects the matter to another ground.
2. **The chain of dispositors** — the planet, the ruler of its sign, the ruler of that one's sign,
   until it closes in a loop. It shows what the matter is subordinate to. `carta.py` prints it.
3. **The aspects to the ruler**, sorted by exactness. Who speaks to it and how strongly.
4. **The derived house** — the other person's chart seen from the client's. The 9th from the 7th
   is the partner's father. It serves to work out *whose* the problem is.
5. **The dignity and accidental state** of everything involved.
6. **Prediction** (module 12) — progressions and transits: *when* this is activated, and whether
   it has already happened or is still to come.

## The stopping rules

Without these, going deeper is only embellishing. They are not negotiable:

- **Stop when three independent instruments converge.** It is confirmed. Going on is vanity.
- **Stop when the next calculation would not change any advice.** If the answer is the same
  whatever you do, you have finished.
- **Stop when you stop calculating and start narrating.** The moment a new layer comes out of your
  imagination rather than out of a position, it is over.
- **Say "this is as far as the chart goes".** It is a complete professional answer.

## The discipline that holds it up

At every layer, separate two things and say so out loud:

- **Data:** "Saturn is at 20°30′ of Capricorn, in the 6th house, with no exact aspects." That is
  astronomy. It is verifiable. It is not open to argument.
- **Inference:** "That suggests a constitution that demands discipline and gives slow results."
  That is interpretation. It is arguable, and must be presented as such.

The deeper you go, the more inference looks like data. **It is not.** The precision of the
calculation does not carry any certainty over to the reading.

## An example of the complete loop

**Layer 0.** "There is something with the father."

**Layer 1.** Where from? Sun square Saturn with 2° of orb, and the 4th house with Capricorn on the
cusp. Two independent factors. Go on.

**Layer 2.** Where is the ruler of the 4th? Saturn, in the 12th house. → *The matter is not
conflict: it is absence, withdrawal, something unsaid.* The sentence has already changed.

**Layer 3.** What aspects that Saturn? Neptune trine, exact. → *An absence with a component of
idealisation or confusion, not of harshness.*

**Layer 4.** Derived house: the 4th from the 4th is the 7th — the father's home, his own ties.
With Mars inside it. → *What is missing comes to the father from his own history.*

**Stop.** Three instruments converge on "absence, not conflict" and the fourth explains the
origin. The next calculation would change nothing of what the client would be told. Stop.

**Result:** from "there is something with the father" to "an absence rather than a clash, with an
idealised or indistinct figure, and with an origin coming from the father's own history".

That is going deeper.
