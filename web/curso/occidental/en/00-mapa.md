# Map of the course

Fourteen modules in four blocks. Each one rests on the one before it.

**Block I — The sky (1-4).** What you are looking at, and how a chart is put up.
1. The sky from one point · 2. The hour · 3. The calculation · 4. The angles

**Block II — The pieces (5-8).** The vocabulary, one piece at a time.
5. Positions · 6. Houses and rulers · 7. Aspects · 8. Dignities

**Block III — The reading (9-11).** Where it stops being vocabulary and the craft begins.
9. Combining and counteracting · 10. Synthesis · 11. Going deeper

And `motor-profundizacion.md`, which is not a module but the reading procedure itself.

**Block IV — The craft (12-14).**
12. Prediction · 13. Ethics and limits · 14. The sidereal zodiac

## What you will be able to do at the end

- Put up a chart by hand, with an ephemeris, without a computer
- Explain where every line of the drawing comes from
- Read a whole chart and write it as running prose, not as a list
- Recognise when a reading holds up and when it is being invented
- Know which calculation to make when an answer comes out blurred
