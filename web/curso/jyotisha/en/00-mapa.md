# Jyotiṣa course — map

Sixteen modules. The first five and the one on ethics are shared with the Western course, because
the astronomy and the craft do not change between traditions.

## Block I — The sky (1-4)

- **01 · The sky from one point** — horizon, meridian, quadrants, hemispheres
- **02 · The hour** — time zones, daylight saving, how an hour is validated
- **03 · The calculation** — sidereal time and angles. In jyotiṣa one step is added: subtracting
  the ayanāṁśa
- **04 · The angles** — the Lagna is the same point of the sky as the Ascendant

## Block II — The pieces (5-10)

- **05 · The rāśis** — the sidereal zodiac and why your Sun changes sign
- **06 · The nakṣatras** — the 27 lunar mansions, their padas and their lords
- **07 · The bhāvas** — whole-sign houses and, above all, their lords
- **08 · The grahas** — the nine, and why the outer planets are not among them
- **09 · Dṛṣṭi** — the aspect by whole sign, without orbs
- **10 · Dignity** — exaltation with its exact degree, nīca-bhaṅga, combustion, gaṇḍānta

## Block III — The instruments (11-12, 14-15)

- **11 · The vargas** — a different complete chart for each matter
- **12 · The daśās** — the clock of a life
- **14 · The kārakas** — the fixed ones and Jaimini's variable ones
- **15 · The yogas** — the combinations, and why they are not to be collected

## Block IV — The craft (13, 16)

- **13 · The craft, ethics and limits** — what is done with this and what is not
- **16 · Going deeper** — from a general finding to its cause, and where you stop

## What you will be able to do at the end

- Put up a Vedic chart and explain where every figure comes from
- Read it by houses, lords, kārakas and dṛṣṭi, not by lists of meanings
- Know which daśā somebody is in and what can be expected of it
- Go to the right varga when an answer comes out blurred
- And recognise when you have stopped calculating and started inventing
