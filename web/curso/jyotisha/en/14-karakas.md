# Module 14 — The kārakas

**Aim:** to know that jyotiṣa has **two sets of significators** for the same matters, and that they
are read together.

## Fixed kārakas

The usual ones: each graha signifies certain things, and that does not change from chart to chart.

| Graha | Signifies |
|---|---|
| Sun | the father, authority, the soul |
| Moon | the mother, the mind, care |
| Mars | siblings, land, courage |
| Mercury | speech, trade, study |
| Jupiter | children, wealth, the guru; the husband in a woman's chart |
| Venus | the wife, pleasure, vehicles |
| Saturn | longevity, hard work, debts |

**How they are used:** for any matter you look at **three things at once**, not one:

1. the **house** of the matter
2. the **lord** of that house
3. the **fixed kāraka** of the matter

For the mother: the 4th house, its lord, and the Moon. If all three say the same thing, it is
firm. If one dissents, there is shading. It is the convergence rule of module 16 applied here.

And a classical warning: **a kāraka inside its own house is considered a hindrance** — *kāraka
bhāva nāśa*. The Moon in the 4th house or Jupiter in the 5th is not always good news.

## Variable kārakas: Jaimini's cara-kārakas

Here is what exists in no other system.

Besides the fixed ones there is a set that **changes in every chart**. You take the seven grahas —
without Rāhu or Ketu in the most used version — and sort them **by the degree they occupy inside
their sign**, highest to lowest. The resulting order assigns the roles:

| | Kāraka | Signifies |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | **Ātmakāraka** (AK) | the soul. The most important of all |
| 2nd | **Amātyakāraka** (AmK) | the mind, the counsellor, the career |
| 3rd | **Bhrātṛkāraka** (BK) | siblings, the guru |
| 4th | **Mātṛkāraka** (MK) | the mother |
| 5th | **Pitṛkāraka** (PiK) | the father |
| 6th | **Putrakāraka** (PK) | children and disciples |
| 7th | **Ñātikāraka** (GK) | obstacles, enmities |

So in a chart the mother is signified by **the Moon** (fixed) and also by **whichever graha holds
the fourth highest degree** (variable). Both are read, and the contrast between them is
information.

## The Ātmakāraka

It is the graha with the highest degree in the whole chart, and it stands for **the soul itself**:
what one has come to do and what one is carrying.

From it comes the **kārakāṁśa**: the sign the Ātmakāraka occupies **in the navāṁśa**, used as a
third ascendant for reading spiritual dharma.

Which graha is the AK says a great deal. A Jupiter as AK is considered a priestly or teaching
soul; a Saturn as AK, a soul that has come to work and to let go.

## Common mistakes

- Using only the house and forgetting the kāraka, or the other way round
- Calculating the cara-kārakas by absolute longitude instead of by degree within the sign
- Taking a matter as settled because one of the three sources says so
