# Module 8 — The grahas

**Aim:** to know who the nine are, what each does, and why Uranus, Neptune and Pluto are not among
them.

## Nine, not ten

| Graha | Function | Fixed kāraka of |
|---|---|---|
| **Sūrya** · Sun | soul, vitality, authority | the father |
| **Candra** · Moon | mind, emotion, need | the mother |
| **Maṅgala** · Mars | energy, courage, cutting | siblings, land |
| **Budha** · Mercury | intelligence, speech, trade | the word |
| **Guru** · Jupiter | wisdom, expansion, dharma | children, husband, wealth |
| **Śukra** · Venus | pleasure, art, union | wife, vehicles |
| **Śani** · Saturn | time, limit, debt, detachment | longevity, servants |
| **Rāhu** | desire, obsession, the foreign, the unusual | — |
| **Ketu** | detachment, mokṣa, what has already been let go | — |

**Rāhu and Ketu are not bodies.** They are the two points where the Moon's orbit cuts the ecliptic,
always opposite each other and always moving retrograde. Eclipses happen there, and that is where
their reputation comes from: they are the ones who "swallow" the Sun and the Moon. In
interpretation they work like shadows — having no substance of their own, they act through
whatever they touch.

## Why the outer planets are not there

Because they cannot be seen. Jyotiṣa is a closed, coherent system built on the seven visible bodies
plus the two nodes, and its whole apparatus — the 120-year daśās, the dignities, the lords of the
signs — is calculated on those nine.

Putting Uranus in breaks the arithmetic: it has no sign of its own, no exaltation, and enters no
daśā. Some modern Indian astrologers use them as shading, but **they are not part of the classical
system** and this course does not include them.

## Benefics and malefics

A basic distinction, though less rigid than it looks.

**Natural benefics:** Jupiter, Venus, Mercury and the waxing Moon.
**Natural malefics:** Saturn, Mars, the Sun, Rāhu, Ketu and the waning Moon.

But **benevolence depends on the Lagna**. A natural malefic ruling a good house can do a great deal
of good, and a benefic ruling a bad house can spoil things. Which is why jyotiṣa speaks of
**functional** benefics, which change with the ascendant.

A rule that saves errors: **first look at which houses a graha rules, then at whether it is benefic
or malefic by nature.**

## The Moon carries more weight than it seems

In jyotiṣa the Moon is not one planet among others. It is **the mind**, and the chart is also read
from it as though it were a second Lagna — the *candra-lagna*. Many readings are done twice: from
the Lagna and from the Moon, and what agrees in both is what is firm.

It is also what starts the clock of the daśās.

## Common mistakes

- Applying "Saturn is bad" without looking at what it rules
- Treating Rāhu and Ketu as planets with bodies
- Reading only from the Lagna and never from the Moon
- Smuggling in the outer planets because you come from Western astrology
