# Module 12 — The daśās

**Aim:** to understand the clock of jyotiṣa, which is what gives it its precision in time.

## The idea

The natal chart says **what** is there. The daśās say **when** it is switched on.

It is a system of chained periods: each graha governs a stretch of years, and inside that stretch
there are sub-stretches, and inside those sub-sub-stretches. You can go down to the scale of days.

The main system is the **vimśottarī**, of 120 years in all:

| Graha | Years |
|---|---|
| Ketu | 7 |
| Venus | 20 |
| Sun | 6 |
| Moon | 10 |
| Mars | 7 |
| Rāhu | 18 |
| Jupiter | 16 |
| Saturn | 19 |
| Mercury | 17 |

Always in that order, in a cycle.

## Where it starts from

**From the Moon's nakṣatra at birth.** The lord of that nakṣatra governs the first daśā, and the
part of the nakṣatra already travelled says how much of that period was used up at birth.

That is why module 6 said the Moon's nakṣatra is the clock. Everything hangs from it. And that is
why an error in the hour that moves the Moon into another nakṣatra **throws a whole life out of
place**.

## The levels

**Mahādaśā** — the major period, 6 to 20 years. It marks the underlying climate of a stage.
**Antardaśā** or bhukti — the sub-period. Months or a few years. The one used most.
**Pratyantardaśā** — the sub-sub. Weeks or months. For sharpening dates.

They are named in a chain: *Mercury-Saturn-Moon* is the pratyantardaśā of the Moon inside the
bhukti of Saturn inside the mahādaśā of Mercury.

## How a period is read

A period **delivers what the graha promises in the chart**, not something new.

To know what the daśā of Venus will bring you have to look, in the natal chart, at:
- which **houses** Venus rules
- which **house** it is lodged in
- what **dignity** it holds
- **who looks at it**
- what the **navāṁśa** says about it

A Venus exalted in a kendra ruling good houses gives a splendid period. The same Venus debilitated
in the 8th gives something quite different. **The daśā does not invent: it activates.**

And it has to be read twice: from the Lagna and from the Moon.

## What has to be resisted

A difficult period **is not a sentence**. It describes what is put on the table and what is asked
for, not what is going to happen. The rule of module 13 applies here in full: tendencies, not
verdicts, and no dates for events.

And transits — *gocara* — are read **on top of** the daśās, never in their place. A hard transit
during a favourable daśā is not the same thing as the same transit during a hard one.

## Exercise

On their chart: which mahādaśā and which bhukti they are in **right now**. Then look in the natal
chart at how that graha stands — houses it rules, lodging, dignity, who looks at it — and write
what could be expected of the period.

And the honest check: look **backwards**. Locate the previous change of mahādaśā and see what
happened in their life around those dates. The past can no longer be accommodated, so it is the
only clean calibration.

## Common mistakes

- Reading the daśā without looking at how that graha stands in the natal chart
- Putting dates on concrete events
- Using transits without daśās
- Forgetting to read from the Moon as well
