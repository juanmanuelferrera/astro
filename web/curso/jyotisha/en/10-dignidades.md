# Module 10 — Dignity and strength

**Aim:** to be able to measure how much each graha weighs, which in jyotiṣa is more precise than in
Western astrology.

## The essential dignities

| State | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Uccha** · exalted | in the sign where it gives its maximum |
| **Mūlatrikoṇa** | the stretch of degrees inside its own sign where it is especially comfortable |
| **Svakṣetra** · own sign | at home: comfortable and with resources |
| **Nīca** · debilitated | in the sign opposite its exaltation: against the grain |

What jyotiṣa sharpens and Western astrology does not: **exaltation has an exact degree of maximum
strength.**

| Graha | Exalted in | Exact degree | Debilitated in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sun | Meṣa | 10° | Tulā |
| Moon | Vṛṣabha | 3° | Vṛścika |
| Mars | Makara | 28° | Karka |
| Mercury | Kanyā | 15° | Mīna |
| Jupiter | Karka | 5° | Makara |
| Venus | Mīna | 27° | Kanyā |
| Saturn | Tulā | 20° | Meṣa |

A Sun at 10° of Meṣa is at its maximum point. At 28° of Meṣa it is still exalted, but less so.

## Nīca-bhaṅga: the debilitation that is cancelled

A debilitated graha is not condemned. If certain conditions are met the debilitation is
**annulled**, and often turns into a greater strength than it would have had without it. It is
called **nīca-bhaṅga rāja-yoga**.

The most accepted conditions: that the lord of the sign where it is debilitated, or the graha that
is exalted in that same sign, sits **in a kendra** from the Lagna or from the Moon.

It is worth checking whenever a nīca turns up, because it changes the reading completely.

## Other things that give or take away strength

**Combustion (asta).** A graha too close to the Sun is burnt and loses its capacity to act. The
margins vary by graha, somewhere around 8-12°.

**Retrogradation (vakrī).** In jyotiṣa this reads differently from Western astrology: a retrograde
graha is often considered **stronger**, not weaker — it is closer to the Earth and its effect is
intensified, though it turns inward.

**Dig-bala** · directional strength. Each graha has a house where it works best by its position in
the sky: Jupiter and Mercury in the 1st, Sun and Mars in the 10th, Saturn in the 7th, Moon and
Venus in the 4th. A graha that is exalted **and** has dig-bala is about as strong as it gets.

**Gaṇḍānta.** The last 3°20′ of Karka, Vṛścika and Mīna — the water-fire junction. It is a karmic
knot: whatever falls there brings an unresolved matter and calls for ritual śānti.

## The question that orders the chart

With this you can answer: **which graha rules here?**

Look at who accumulates most: lord of the Lagna, essential dignity, angular house, dig-bala, gazes
received, and part in yogas. That graha is the key, and it is where the reading begins.

## Common mistakes

- Seeing a nīca and giving the matter up for lost without checking the bhaṅga
- Forgetting combustion, which is silent and annuls a great deal
- Applying the Western reading of retrogradation
- Stopping at essential dignity and never looking at the house
